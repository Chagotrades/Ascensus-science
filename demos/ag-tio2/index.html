Scientific notes

Source. Photocatalytic oxidation of ceftiofur sodium under UV–visible irradiation using plasmonic porous Ag-TiO₂ nanospheres. J. Ind. Eng. Chem. 105 (2022) 384–392. doi:10.1016/j.jiec.2021.09.038

From the paper: ~250 nm porous anatase nanospheres of ~10 nm particles (green route); 3–4 nm plasmonic Ag; photoluminescence shows Ag suppresses e⁻/h⁺ recombination; 92% vs 71% ceftiofur oxidation in 90 min; non-toxic products (LC-MS, Microtox).

Schematic choices (for author validation):

  • Molecules are drawn ×6 larger than real size. Ceftiofur structure: PubChem CID 6328657 (3D conformer, neutral form).
  • Eg ≈ 3.2 eV is a typical anatase value, not taken from this paper.
  • •OH is shown as the main oxidant; O₂•⁻ formation at Ag is shown as a secondary path. Confirm with the paper's scavenger experiments.
  • Electron transfer is shown as TiO₂ → Ag (electron trap). Plasmonic hot-electron injection under visible light is not shown.
  • Molecule fragmentation is illustrative; it does not represent the specific LC-MS products.
  • Pore structure and Ag distribution are artistic approximations.
