Photocatalytic oxidation of ceftiofur sodium using plasmonic porous Ag-TiO₂ nanospheres

J. Ind. Eng. Chem. 105 (2022) 384–392 · doi:10.1016/j.jiec.2021.09.038

Explore in 3D → Watch the 1-minute video

In one minute

Porous anatase TiO₂ nanospheres (~250 nm), built from ~10 nm particles and decorated with 3–4 nm plasmonic silver, oxidize the antibiotic ceftiofur under white light.

Silver traps the photo-generated electrons, so the holes survive long enough to form hydroxyl radicals that break the antibiotic down into non-toxic products.

92%ceftiofur oxidized in 90 min with porous Ag–TiO₂
71%with Ag on commercial TiO₂ (P25), same conditions
